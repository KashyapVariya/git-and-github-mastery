## Why it matters

If you maintain any repository that gets outside contributions, you end up typing the same few responses over and over: "Thanks for the PR — please add a test," "This is a duplicate of #123," "Please fill out the issue template." Saved replies turn each of those into a one-click insert.

## The core idea

A saved reply is a small template — a title plus a body — stored on your GitHub account. It's available from the comment box on any issue, pull request, or discussion, via a small icon in the toolbar. Selecting one drops its text into the comment field, ready to edit before you post, or send as-is.

## Setting one up

1. Go to your account settings → **Saved replies**.
2. Add a title (just for your own reference, e.g. "Needs a test") and the reply body.
3. From now on, the comment box on any issue or PR shows a saved-replies icon — click it to insert.

<Callout type="tip">
Saved replies are personal, not shared with your team automatically. For a whole team's set of canned responses, some projects instead keep a `SUPPORT.md` or a pinned discussion the team copies from — saved replies are best for your own individual habits.
</Callout>

## Try it yourself

Create a saved reply for the most common thing you find yourself typing on issues or PRs — even something as small as "Thanks for reporting this — I'll take a look soon!" — and use it on your next comment.
