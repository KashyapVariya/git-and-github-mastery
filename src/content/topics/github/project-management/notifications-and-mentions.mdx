## Why it matters

Left on defaults, GitHub notifications can turn into noise fast — every comment on every repo you've ever touched. Understanding *why* you got notified (and how to control it) is the difference between notifications being useful and you ignoring them entirely.

## Why you get notified

You're subscribed to a thread — an issue, a pull request, a discussion — for one of a few reasons:

- You **created** it, or **commented** on it
- You were **@mentioned** by name
- You were **assigned** or **requested for review**
- You're explicitly **watching** the whole repository

Each of these can be tuned separately in your notification settings: some people want an email for review requests but nothing else; some want everything to land in the GitHub notifications inbox only.

## @mentions

Typing `@username` in a comment, issue, or PR pings that specific person, regardless of whether they're subscribed to the thread otherwise. Mention a team (`@org/team-name`) to notify every member at once — useful for "someone from platform, can you look at this?" without picking a person.

## Managing the firehose

- **Per-repository control:** click "Watch" on any repository and choose "Participating and @mentions only" instead of "All Activity" — this alone removes most noise from busy repos you don't own.
- **The notifications inbox** (github.com/notifications) lets you mark things read, unsubscribe from a specific thread, or "Done" it — separate from your email.
- **Custom routing:** GitHub lets you send notifications from specific organizations to a different email address than your personal ones, handy for keeping work and personal notifications apart.

<Callout type="tip">
If a thread is dragging on and no longer concerns you, click "Unsubscribe" on that specific issue or PR instead of muting the whole repository — you'll still get pinged again if someone @mentions you directly.
</Callout>

## Try it yourself

Open your notification settings (github.com/settings/notifications) and change your default watching behavior for repositories to "Participating and @mentions only."
